# User guide

Short task guides by role. Menus follow the main navigation: Dashboard, Course Catalog, My Learning, Training Management, Employee Management, Competency Validation, Certifications, Compliance Center, Instructor Portal, Reports and Analytics, Notifications, Billing, Administration, Settings.

## Learners (all staff)
| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| See what I must complete and by when | **My Learning → Assigned training** (due dates and status) |
| Take a course | **My Learning** → Start/Resume. Lessons save progress automatically; knowledge checks do not count toward your score. |
| Take the final exam | Appears when all required lessons are complete. Answers save as you go; time limits and attempt limits are shown first. |
| Review a failed exam | Open the attempt. Correct answers show once you pass or have used all attempts; the **AI study assistant** can explain an incorrect answer then. |
| Register for a class | **My Learning → Classes → Open classes → Register** |
| Download a certificate | **Certifications** → PDF. Anyone can verify it with the QR code. Certificates are internal training records — not licenses or accredited CE. |
| Evaluate a course | Course overview page after completion (1–5 stars and comments) |
| Choose email/text alerts | **Settings → Notifications** (text alerts only if your organization enables them) |

## Supervisors
- **Employee Management** shows your direct reports and their training status.
- **Competency Validation**: start an evaluation, record each criterion, attach evidence (no patient images), then sign with your password. Signed evaluations cannot be changed.
- **Training Management → Expirations** lists your team's upcoming expirations.

## Training directors and instructors
- **Training Management**: assign courses (person, position, facility or department), set requirements, extend due dates with an audited reason, review expirations.
- **Instructor Portal**: grade written answers, authorize retakes, schedule classes, register attendees, take attendance, message a class or course, manage instructor profiles and credentials.
- **Course Catalog → Manage courses**: build organization courses (Enterprise) — modules, lessons, media uploads, question banks — then submit for review. Publishing requires the recorded approval chain.

## Organization and facility administrators
- **Administration**: organization profile, facilities and departments, users and invitations, roles (custom roles on Enterprise), notification settings, integrations/API keys (Enterprise), and the **audit log** with chain verification.
- **Employee Management**: add employees (onboarding assigns required training automatically), licenses with document copies, facility assignments.
- **Compliance Center** (Professional+): regulatory rules that apply to each facility, compliance by facility and employee, decisions and mappings to training. Rules marked *Needs review* are not verified law.
- **Reports and Analytics**: 16 reports with PDF/Excel/CSV export; the **Analytics dashboard** (Enterprise) with filters and trends.
- **Billing**: plan, usage, invoices and payment history; upgrades through Stripe Checkout when enabled.

## Platform administrators
- **Platform console**: tenant approvals and status, plans and custom pricing, platform-wide statistics.
- **Course library**: review and approve courses (Review → Clinical (credentials required) → Compliance).
- **Regulatory library**: add sources, edit rules with change summaries, verification queue.

## Getting help
Contact your organization administrator first. Report suspected abuse to the **Florida Abuse Hotline 1-800-96-ABUSE**; in an emergency call 911.
